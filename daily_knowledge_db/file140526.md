# 1. Core Principles

```yaml
core_principles:
  - id: CP001
    name: Localized Trust
    statement: >
      In low-trust societies, trust is restricted to small in-groups
      rather than generalized across society.
    implications:
      - Institutions weaken
      - Nepotism increases
      - Informal networks dominate
      - Civic cooperation declines

  - id: CP002
    name: Survival Intelligence Dominates Civic Ethics
    statement: >
      Long exposure to unreliable systems causes tactical survival behavior
      to override contribution-oriented ethics.
    implications:
      - Short-termism
      - Opportunism
      - Rule-bending normalization
      - Cynicism transmission

  - id: CP003
    name: Cultural Feedback Loop
    statement: >
      Cynicism teaches manipulation; manipulation destroys trust;
      destroyed trust justifies further cynicism.
    implications:
      - Recursive social decay
      - Self-reinforcing distrust
      - Institutional fragility

  - id: CP004
    name: Bounded Opportunism Expands
    statement: >
      Behaviors initially used defensively against hostile systems eventually
      spread into family and close relationships.
    implications:
      - Family mistrust
      - Transactional relationships
      - Emotional fragmentation

  - id: CP005
    name: Competence Creates Sovereignty
    statement: >
      The less dependent one is on arbitrary systems, the greater the freedom
      from manipulation and social extraction.
    implications:
      - Skill accumulation matters
      - Financial buffers matter
      - Optionality matters
      - Technical leverage matters

  - id: CP006
    name: Calmness Is Competitive Advantage
    statement: >
      In emotionally noisy environments, emotional regulation itself becomes
      a strategic edge.
    implications:
      - Better decision quality
      - Reduced impulsivity
      - Long-term compounding
```

# 7. Operational Rules / Checklists

## Life Operating Rules

```yaml
life_rules:

  - rule: Build micro-trust networks slowly
  - rule: Never confuse politeness with trustworthiness
  - rule: Reduce dependence on arbitrary systems
  - rule: Document important interactions
  - rule: Preserve internal ethics while adapting externally
  - rule: Keep emotional regulation as strategic priority
  - rule: Move quietly after success
  - rule: Choose incentive environments carefully
```

---

## Trading Checklist

```yaml
trading_checklist:

  pre_trade:
    - Is this trade part of the tested system?
    - Am I changing rules emotionally?
    - Am I reacting to boredom or fear?

  during_trade:
    - Do not fiddle without predefined trigger
    - Observe discomfort without acting
    - Respect probabilistic uncertainty

  post_trade:
    - Evaluate process, not outcome
    - Record emotional state
    - Detect novelty-seeking impulses
```

---

## Anti-Self-Sabotage Rules

```yaml
anti_self_sabotage_rules:

  - After profitable periods:
      mandatory_action:
        - Reduce discretionary experimentation
        - Increase adherence to baseline system

  - Before strategy changes:
      mandatory_action:
        - Sandbox test only
        - No live deployment without evidence

  - During emotional turbulence:
      mandatory_action:
        - Delay decisions
        - Use predefined rules only

  - When feeling urge to interfere:
      self_question:
        - "Am I improving expectancy or reducing discomfort?"
```
