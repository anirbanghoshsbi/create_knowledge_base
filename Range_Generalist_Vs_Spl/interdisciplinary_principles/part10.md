operational_rules:

  trading_rules:

    - rule: "Use outside-view forecasting."
      checklist:
        - What historical reference class resembles this?
        - What usually happens in similar regimes?
        - Am I overfocused on local details?

    - rule: "Never rely on one analogy."
      implementation:
        - Compare:
            - inflation analogies
            - crisis analogies
            - liquidity cycles
            - behavioral episodes
            - volatility expansions

    - rule: "Build structural rather than narrative models."
      checklist:
        - What are the causal mechanisms?
        - What incentives drive behavior?
        - What feedback loops exist?

    - rule: "Preserve execution stability."
      implementation:
        - Require evidence before changing systems.
        - Separate conceptual research from production trading.
        - Maintain predefined rules.

  machine_learning_rules:

    - rule: "Train broad relational representations."
      checklist:
        - Can model transfer across domains?
        - Is representation structural?
        - Is generalization robust?

    - rule: "Use reference-class validation."
      implementation:
        - Compare across:
            - market regimes
            - datasets
            - structural conditions
            - adversarial scenarios

  decision_rules:

    - rule: "Force outside-view reasoning."
      checklist:
        - What comparable cases exist?
        - What base rates apply?
        - Am I trapped in local detail?

    - rule: "Generate multiple analogies."

        - Ask:
            - What else behaves like this?
            - What hidden structure repeats?
            - Which distant system resembles this dynamically?
