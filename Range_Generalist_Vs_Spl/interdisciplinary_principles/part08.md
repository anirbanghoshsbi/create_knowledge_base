core_principles:

  - id: CP56
    principle: "Immediate performance is often a poor proxy for long-term learning."
    explanation: >
      Rapid short-term success frequently reflects shallow procedural fluency
      rather than durable conceptual understanding.

  - id: CP57
    principle: "Desirable difficulties improve durable learning."
    explanation: >
      Struggle, retrieval effort, spacing, and conceptual problem solving
      strengthen long-term retention and transfer capability.

  - id: CP58
    principle: "Generation enhances understanding."
    explanation: >
      Attempting to generate answers independently,
      even incorrect ones,
      improves subsequent learning.

  - id: CP59
    principle: "Hint-heavy learning creates fragile knowledge."
    explanation: >
      Excessive guidance improves immediate performance
      but weakens abstraction and independent reasoning.

  - id: CP60
    principle: "Conceptual learning outperforms procedural memorization."
    explanation: >
      Learners who understand underlying structure
      outperform those who merely memorize algorithms.

  - id: CP61
    principle: "Spacing strengthens long-term retention."
    explanation: >
      Distributed struggle over time improves memory consolidation
      and transfer ability.

  - id: CP62
    principle: "Retrieval difficulty strengthens learning."
    explanation: >
      Effortful recall improves durable encoding
      even when retrieval initially fails.

  - id: CP63
    principle: "Students systematically misjudge effective learning."
    explanation: >
      Humans mistake ease, fluency, and immediate success
      for genuine mastery.

  - id: CP64
    principle: "The best teachers often appear worse in the short term."
    explanation: >
      Instructors who force deeper conceptual engagement
      may reduce immediate performance
      while increasing long-term capability.

  - id: CP65
    principle: "Deep learning requires connection-making."
    explanation: >
      Knowledge becomes transferable
      when linked across contexts and abstractions.

operational_rules:

  trading_rules:

    - rule: "Do not confuse short-term success with robust edge."
      checklist:
        - Has this survived multiple regimes?
        - Is this structurally causal?
        - Would this survive stress conditions?
        - Am I optimizing for recent outcomes?

    - rule: "Use desirable difficulties in research."
      implementation:
        - Stress-test assumptions.
        - Simulate adverse scenarios.
        - Analyze failed trades deeply.
        - Use out-of-sample validation.

    - rule: "Practice retrieval-based reasoning."
      checklist:
        - Can I reconstruct this system from memory?
        - Can I explain it without notes?
        - Can I apply it in new environments?

    - rule: "Preserve conceptual thinking."
      implementation:
        - Focus on:
            - liquidity
            - incentives
            - positioning
            - reflexivity
            - volatility structure
            - behavioral flows

  machine_learning_rules:

    - rule: "Optimize for generalization, not training fluency."
      checklist:
        - Does model survive unseen data?
        - Is learning transferable?
        - Is validation sufficiently difficult?

    - rule: "Introduce controlled difficulty."
      implementation:
        - Use adversarial testing.
        - Use distribution shifts.
        - Avoid over-curated datasets.

  learning_rules:

    - rule: "Prefer generation over passive review."
      checklist:
        - Did I attempt solution first?
        - Did I retrieve from memory?
        - Did I struggle productively?

    - rule: "Use spacing deliberately."
      implementation:
        - Revisit concepts after delay.
        - Rotate subjects periodically.
        - Use cumulative review.

    - rule: "Allow confusion temporarily."
      reminders:
        - Confusion is not failure.
        - Ease may signal shallow learning.
        - Productive frustration compounds later.

high_level_summary:

  summary: >
    This section argues that durable adaptive learning
    is fundamentally counterintuitive.

    The strongest long-term learning emerges from:
      - struggle
      - retrieval
      - spacing
      - generation
      - conceptual connection-making

    while immediate fluency and easy progress
    often create fragile illusions of mastery.

    Deep expertise therefore compounds slowly,
    painfully,
    and transferably.
