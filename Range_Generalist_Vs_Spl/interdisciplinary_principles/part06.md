core_principles:

  - id: CP38
    principle: "Sampling periods are foundational, not optional."
    explanation: >
      Early exploration across multiple skills and domains
      builds adaptive capability and improves eventual specialization quality.

  - id: CP39
    principle: "Breadth accelerates later mastery."
    explanation: >
      Exposure to diverse instruments, methods, and contexts
      creates transferable abstractions that improve advanced performance.

  - id: CP40
    principle: "Exceptional performers often specialize later."
    explanation: >
      Many elite performers first develop broad exposure,
      then intensify effort once intrinsic motivation emerges.

  - id: CP41
    principle: "Intrinsic pull matters more than forced specialization."
    explanation: >
      Sustainable expertise emerges when the learner gravitates naturally
      toward a domain rather than being externally forced into it.

  - id: CP42
    principle: "Experimentation creates adaptive fluency."
    explanation: >
      Trial-and-error exploration builds flexible understanding
      superior to rigid procedural memorization.

  - id: CP43
    principle: "Multi-domain exposure increases creative recombination."
    explanation: >
      Diverse skills interact to create novel capabilities and innovation.

  - id: CP44
    principle: "Over-structured early training can suppress adaptive growth."
    explanation: >
      Excessive rigid instruction early in development
      may reduce exploration and transfer capacity.

  - id: CP45
    principle: "Generalized skill scaffolding transfers across modalities."
    explanation: >
      Learning one domain deeply can partially transfer
      to adjacent domains when abstract structures overlap.

  - id: CP46
    principle: "Adaptive laboratories outperform narrow pipelines."
    explanation: >
      Environments encouraging experimentation and role fluidity
      produce greater innovation.

high_level_summary:

  summary: >
    This section argues that breadth and experimentation
    are not obstacles to mastery,
    but foundational ingredients of adaptive expertise.

    The strongest long-term performers frequently:
      - sample broadly
      - experiment extensively
      - specialize later
      - integrate across domains
      - maintain flexibility

    Adaptive excellence emerges not from repetition alone,
    but from broad transferable scaffolding combined later with focused depth.
