operational_rules:

  trading_rules:

    - rule: "Design environments that support discipline."
      checklist:
        - What contexts trigger impulsivity?
        - What market regimes destabilize me?
        - What environments improve execution consistency?

    - rule: "Use conditional behavioral models."
      implementation:
        - Track:
            - emotional states
            - volatility regimes
            - boredom levels
            - overconfidence phases
            - drawdown behavior

    - rule: "Preserve exploratory systems safely."
      implementation:
        - Sandbox experimentation.
        - Separate live execution from conceptual research.
        - Delay deployment of new frameworks.

    - rule: "Avoid premature optimization."
      checklist:
        - Am I locking into one framework too early?
        - Is exploration still producing information?
        - Am I overfitting current conditions?

  machine_learning_rules:

    - rule: "Train across contexts."
      checklist:
        - Are regimes diverse?
        - Does model generalize conditionally?
        - Are context shifts represented?

    - rule: "Preserve adaptive flexibility."
      implementation:
        - Multi-regime validation
        - Transfer learning
        - Continual learning systems

  decision_rules:

    - rule: "Use test-and-learn identity development."
      checklist:
        - What small experiment can I run?
        - What possible self should I explore?
        - What low-risk test generates information?

    - rule: "Think conditionally, not globally."
      implementation:
        - Replace:
            - "Am I disciplined?"
          with:
            - "Under what conditions am I disciplined?"

high_level_summary:

  summary: >
    This section argues that:
      - personality is dynamic,
      - behavior is context dependent,
      - self-knowledge emerges experimentally,
      - and adaptive careers evolve through iterative exploration.

    Rather than rigidly optimizing for a fixed identity,
    individuals should:
      - test possible selves,
      - recalibrate continuously,
      - preserve optionality,
      - and adapt environments to support alignment.

    Long-term adaptive success therefore emerges
    less from fixed planning
    and more from intelligent iterative experimentation.
