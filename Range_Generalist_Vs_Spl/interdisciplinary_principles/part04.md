core_principles:

  - id: CP21
    principle: "Modern complexity rewards cognitive flexibility."
    explanation: >
      As environments become more interconnected and abstract,
      rigid pattern repetition becomes insufficient.
    implications:
      - adaptability gains value
      - transfer learning becomes essential
      - abstraction becomes a survival skill

  - id: CP22
    principle: "Conceptual thinking enables transfer across domains."
    explanation: >
      Abstract classification systems allow humans
      to apply knowledge outside direct experience.
    mechanisms:
      - analogical reasoning
      - conceptual mapping
      - symbolic abstraction
      - flexible categorization

  - id: CP23
    principle: "Modern work changes cognition itself."
    explanation: >
      Exposure to complex, self-directed, nonrepetitive environments
      increases cognitive flexibility and abstract reasoning capacity.

  - id: CP24
    principle: "Range acts as a circuit breaker against cognitive entrenchment."
    explanation: >
      External perspectives interrupt habitual responses
      and prevent overreliance on stale heuristics.

  - id: CP25
    principle: "Adaptive thinkers maintain multiple active identity streams."
    explanation: >
      People who successfully transition across careers
      preserve optionality and multiple developmental paths.

  - id: CP26
    principle: "Abstraction liberates thought from direct experience."
    explanation: >
      Modern cognition increasingly relies on conceptual systems
      rather than concrete lived repetition alone.

  - id: CP27
    principle: "The wicked world requires deriving rules, not memorizing them."
    explanation: >
      In dynamic environments, rules are incomplete,
      evolving, or hidden entirely.

  - id: CP28
    principle: "Knowledge transfer is the central skill of modernity."
    explanation: >
      Competitive advantage increasingly comes from
      applying ideas across domains.

operational_rules:

  trading_rules:

    - rule: "Treat markets as evolving conceptual systems."
      checklist:
        - What hidden structure drives this?
        - Is this regime-specific?
        - What assumptions no longer hold?
        - What second-order effects exist?

    - rule: "Use circuit breakers against entrenchment."
      implementation:
        - Review opposing theses weekly.
        - Analyze failed trades structurally.
        - Rotate perspectives:
            - macro
            - sentiment
            - volatility
            - liquidity
            - positioning

    - rule: "Preserve optionality."
      implementation:
        - Maintain multiple research streams.
        - Avoid identity fusion with one strategy.
        - Keep exploratory capital separate.

    - rule: "Focus on transferable principles."
      checklist:
        - Does this survive regime shifts?
        - Is this causal or merely historical?
        - Can this generalize across assets?

  machine_learning_rules:

    - rule: "Optimize for representation quality."
      checklist:
        - Does the model capture structure?
        - Can features transfer across regimes?
        - Is this learning abstraction or memorization?

    - rule: "Use ensemble cognition."
      implementation:
        - Combine:
            - technical signals
            - macro signals
            - sentiment
            - behavioral features
            - volatility structure

  decision_rules:

    - rule: "Infer rules rather than assume them."
      checklist:
        - Are rules changing?
        - Is feedback reliable?
        - What hidden incentives exist?
        - What variables are unobserved?

    - rule: "Maintain parallel developmental tracks."
      implementation:
        - Continue AI research.
        - Continue trading development.
        - Continue systems learning.
        - Continue interdisciplinary synthesis.

    required humans to:

    adaptive abstraction increasingly dominates rigid specialization.
