core_principles:

  - id: CP14
    principle: "Expert intuition is compressed pattern recognition."
    explanation: >
      Experts do not possess magical memory.
      They recognize recurring structures and compress information into chunks.
    examples:
      - chess masters
      - quarterbacks
      - musicians
      - restaurant servers

  - id: CP15
    principle: "Chunking works only in stable patterned environments."
    explanation: >
      Pattern-based expertise collapses when structures become unfamiliar.
    evidence:
      - Chess masters fail on random board layouts.
      - Savants struggle with atonal music.
      - Experts fail under altered rules.

  - id: CP16
    principle: "Strategic synthesis becomes more valuable than tactical execution."
    explanation: >
      As machines automate tactical pattern recognition,
      human advantage shifts toward:
        - orchestration
        - abstraction
        - adaptation
        - integration
        - framing

  - id: CP17
    principle: "Open-world problems reward broad integration."
    explanation: >
      Complex adaptive environments require combining partial,
      uncertain, and evolving information streams.
    examples:
      - StarCraft
      - entrepreneurship
      - macro trading
      - scientific innovation

  - id: CP18
    principle: "Repeated rewards create cognitive rigidity."
    explanation: >
      Success from one narrow solution causes fixation
      and reduces discovery of broader principles.
    mechanism:
      - reinforcement loops
      - local optimization
      - exploitation lock-in

  - id: CP19
    principle: "Creative breakthroughs emerge from domain intersections."
    explanation: >
      Transformational innovation frequently occurs
      when concepts from unrelated fields combine.
    examples:
      - Claude Shannon
      - Steve Jobs
      - Nobel laureates
      - polymath scientists

  - id: CP20
    principle: "Breadth amplifies creativity rather than diluting expertise."
    explanation: >
      External interests strengthen rather than weaken
      high-level creative performance.

operational_rules:

  trading_rules:

    - rule: "Do not rely solely on pattern recognition."
      checklist:
        - Is this structurally causal?
        - Could this regime change invalidate it?
        - Am I overfitting recent conditions?

    - rule: "Preserve strategic thinking."
      implementation:
        - Review macro structure weekly.
        - Study cross-market interactions.
        - Analyze behavioral feedback loops.

    - rule: "Separate exploration from execution."
      checklist:
        - Is this hypothesis tested?
        - Is this production-ready?
        - Is this intellectual curiosity or validated edge?

    - rule: "Avoid cognitive entrenchment."
      implementation:
        - Periodically challenge assumptions.
        - Rotate analytical perspectives.
        - Study contradictory evidence.

  machine_learning_rules:

    - rule: "Optimize for robustness."
      checklist:
        - Does model survive distribution shifts?
        - Can it generalize outside training regime?
        - Are features structurally meaningful?

    - rule: "Use human-AI centaur thinking."
      implementation:
        - Let AI handle tactical computation.
        - Preserve human role in:
            - framing
            - strategy
            - interpretation
            - adaptation

  learning_rules:

    - rule: "Build transferable abstractions."
      implementation:
        - Extract invariant principles.
        - Compare across fields.
        - Maintain concept graph.

    - rule: "Keep one foot outside your field."
      checklist:
        - What unrelated field informs this?
        - What assumptions are invisible internally?
        - What analogy changes perspective?
