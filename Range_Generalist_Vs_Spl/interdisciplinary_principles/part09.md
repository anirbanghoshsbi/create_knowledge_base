    principle: "Interleaving improves flexible expertise."
    explanation: >

      structural differences and match appropriate strategies dynamically.

  - id: CP68

high_level_summary:

  summary: >
    This section argues that adaptive intelligence
    depends on abstraction, interleaving, and analogical reasoning.

    Deep expertise emerges when learners:
      - classify problems structurally
      - transfer concepts across domains
      - tolerate varied difficult exposure
      - reason relationally
      - adapt strategies dynamically

    Innovation frequently emerges
    not from deeper repetition inside one domain,
    but from leaving the domain entirely
    and returning with new conceptual tools.
