operational_rules:

  trading_rules:

    - rule: "Distinguish strategic adaptation from impulsive switching."
      checklist:
        - Is edge statistically degrading?
        - Is regime structure changing?
        - Am I reacting emotionally?
        - Has this been tested sufficiently?

    - rule: "Use failure diagnostically."
      checklist:
        - Was this mismatch or execution error?
        - What information did failure reveal?
        - What assumptions broke?

    - rule: "Preserve exploration separately."
      implementation:
        - Dedicated sandbox capital.
        - Separate research environment.
        - Delayed production deployment.

    - rule: "Optimize for fit, not ideology."
      implementation:
        - Align methods with:
            - temperament
            - cognition
            - energy patterns
            - behavioral tendencies

  machine_learning_rules:

    - rule: "Maintain exploration-exploitation balance."
      checklist:
        - Are we stuck in local minima?
        - Is exploration budget sufficient?
        - Is adaptation constrained?

    - rule: "Treat anomalies as search signals."
      implementation:
        - Analyze failures deeply.
        - Track edge-case behavior.
        - Explore representation weaknesses.

  decision_rules:

    - rule: "Quit strategically, not emotionally."
      checklist:
        - Is continuation compounding value?
        - Is this still aligned?
        - What opportunity cost exists?

    - rule: "Allow identity evolution."
      implementation:
        - Preserve optionality.
        - Continue broad exploration.
        - Reevaluate fit periodically.

high_level_summary:

  summary: >
    This section argues that excessive glorification of grit
    ignores the importance of match quality,
    adaptive switching,
    and exploratory self-discovery.

    High performers frequently:
      - explore broadly,
      - switch paths,
      - fail repeatedly,
      - refine identity iteratively,
      - and eventually converge toward intrinsically aligned work.

    Exploration is not the opposite of mastery.
    It is often the path to it.
