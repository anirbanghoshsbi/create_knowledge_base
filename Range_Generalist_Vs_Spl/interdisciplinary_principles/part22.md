core_principles:

  - id: CP196
    principle: "Incomplete data can create false confidence."
    explanation: >
      Humans often make strong conclusions
      using only visible data
      while ignoring missing information.

  - id: CP197
    principle: "Quantitative rigor alone does not guarantee good decisions."
    explanation: >
      Numerical analysis can fail catastrophically
      when underlying assumptions or relevant variables are misunderstood.

  - id: CP198
    principle: "Wicked problems often require qualitative judgment."
    explanation: >
      In highly uncertain environments,
      intuition,
      interpretation,

  - id: CP199
    principle: "Humans become psychologically attached to familiar tools."
    explanation: >
      Repeated successful use of methods
      creates identity-level attachment
      that impairs adaptation.

  - id: CP200
    principle: "Overlearned behavior becomes dangerous under regime change."
    explanation: >

      may fail catastrophically
      outside familiar conditions.

  - id: CP201
    principle: "Sensemaking is superior to rigid decision attachment."
    explanation: >
      Adaptive thinkers continuously reinterpret environments
      rather than defending prior conclusions.

  - id: CP202
    principle: "Procedural conformity can suppress critical dissent."
    explanation: >
      Strong institutional cultures
      often silence weak but important signals.

  - id: CP203
    principle: "Hidden variables invalidate apparent certainty."
    explanation: >
      Missing causal structure
      can make statistical conclusions misleading.

  - id: CP204
    principle: "Can-do cultures become fragile in novel environments."
    explanation: >

  - id: CP205

      previously successful methods,

operational_rules:

  trading_rules:

    - rule: "Question missing data."
      checklist:
        - What data is absent?
        - What variables remain unobserved?
        - What assumptions does this model hide?

    - rule: "Treat backtests as conditional."
      checklist:
        - What regime assumptions exist?
        - What hidden variables could invalidate this?
        - Is this survivorship-biased?

    - rule: "Drop familiar tools when structure changes."
      implementation:
        - Define:
            - regime invalidation criteria
            - structural failure signals
            - mandatory reassessment triggers

    - rule: "Use sensemaking, not ideological decision defense."
      checklist:
        - Am I defending identity or adapting?
        - What changed?
        - What evidence no longer fits?

  machine_learning_rules:

    - rule: "Expect hidden-variable failure."
      checklist:
        - Does model assume stationarity?
        - Are OOD scenarios tested?
        - What causal structure is missing?

    - rule: "Combine quantitative and qualitative diagnostics."
      implementation:
        - Statistical monitoring
        - Human interpretability
        - Regime-awareness
        - Structural anomaly analysis

  decision_rules:

    - rule: "Separate tools from identity."
      checklist:
        - Am I emotionally attached to this method?
        - Would I choose this fresh today?
        - Is this habit or adaptation?

    - rule: "Prioritize sensemaking over certainty."
      implementation:
        - Maintain:
            - dynamic interpretation
            - weakly held models
            - ongoing reassessment
            - adaptive improvisation

high_level_summary:

  summary: >
    This section argues that:
      - incomplete data creates false certainty,
      - procedural rigor can become maladaptive,
      - and adaptive systems fail when individuals
        cannot abandon familiar tools.

    The Challenger tragedy demonstrates:
      - the danger of hidden variables,
      - the limits of quantitative certainty,
      - and the psychological difficulty
        of abandoning trusted procedures.

    Adaptive thinkers therefore:
      - treat models as conditional,
      - improvise under uncertainty,
      - separate identity from tools,
      - and prioritize sensemaking over rigid certainty.
